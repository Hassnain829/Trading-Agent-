Position sizing

Exposure & daily limits

0 turns a limit off. Hitting the loss limit or the profit target stops new entries until the next trading day (17:00 New York); the day's start equity and a tripped limit survive restarts. New trades are also refused when today's loss plus the risk of every open stop could exceed the loss limit.

Protection

Forex closes Friday 17:00 New York time (—). 0 hours turns the entry pause off. The engine also always tells the AI today's USD direction and penalises trades that fight a broad USD move.

Currency cap: e.g. 2.5% lets two 1% trades be long USD at once (EURUSD sell + USDJPY buy) but not a third. Cooldown: after a losing trade, no new trade in the same direction on that symbol for this long. 0 turns either off.

Strategy

Scalping trades from the London open to midday New York only. Rules find an M5 RSI pullback in the direction of the D1 trend (M15 must agree); the AI confirms or vetoes it; the engine closes it at SL, TP or the time stop. Vetoed and penalty-blocked setups are followed as shadow trades so you can see whether the AI's vetoes help.
