      How the bot decides

      click to read
      1. Daily trend. Only trades in the direction of the daily trend (EMA 200 and its slope, confirmed by EMA 20/50).
      2. Pullback. Waits for price to pull back on the 5-minute chart (RSI dips below 40 in an uptrend, above 60 in a downtrend).
      3. Turn. Enters only when the pullback turns back in the trend direction, and skips setups where price is already stretched.
      4. AI + learning agent. The AI reviews the setup with the wider market picture and says yes or no. The learning agent then decides take or skip, using the AI's answer and what similar setups have earned on your demo and shadow trades (it follows the AI until it has 30 results per strategy). Skipped setups are followed as “shadow trades”, so every decision gets a reward or a penalty.
      5. Trade. Stop behind the recent swing, target 1.5× the risk, closed after 60 minutes at most. Your Safety limits apply to every step.

      The intraday strategy runs at the same time on 15-minute candles: it trades a break and retest of yesterday's high/low or the Asian range (target 2× the risk, up to 6 hours), with the same AI, its own learning agent and the same safety limits.

      Daily limits

      The trading day starts at 17:00 New York time. 0 turns a limit off.

      New trades are also refused when today's loss plus the risk of every open stop could pass the loss limit.

      4

      Account protection

      Measured from the highest equity your account has reached, across days and restarts.

      After a withdrawal, reset too: a withdrawal looks like a loss.

      5

      Strategies and trading hours

      Both rules strategies run at the same time, 24/7 by default: every session (Asia, London, New York) on all your pairs.

      The strategies trade independently: each holds at most one trade per pair, and they may hold opposite sides of a pair (hedging accounts). Each has its own position limit and currency caps; the daily loss limit and the kill-switch cover both together.

      6

      Safety filters

      Situations where the bot stays out. Skipped setups are followed as shadow trades, so the Scorecard shows whether each filter pays.

      Currency limit example: 2.5% lets two 1% trades both bet on the US dollar (sell EURUSD + buy USDJPY), but not a third. 0 turns it off.
